Featured Project

TOI-2049 b — Radial-Velocity Analysis

● In progress · Preliminary

An independent research project investigating archival radial-velocity observations associated with the exoplanet TOI-2049 b. The project explores how periodic stellar motion can be analyzed to study planetary signals — from raw measurements to an orbital model.

TOI-2049 b · radial velocity preliminary
observed RV
model fit
  • Worked with a dataset of 49 radial-velocity observations
  • Organized and validated measurements and uncertainties
  • Built radial-velocity vs. time visualizations
  • Ran an independent search for periodicity
  • Recovered a candidate period of ≈ 5.304 days
  • Phase-folded the observations
  • Performed a weighted sinusoidal RV fit
  • Obtained a preliminary semi-amplitude of ≈ 132 m/s
  • Tested a Keplerian orbital model
  • Compared sinusoidal vs. Keplerian models
  • Began residual and outlier analysis
  • Working toward a formal research manuscript
